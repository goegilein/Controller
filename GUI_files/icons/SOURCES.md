# Stylesheet images

`check.svg`, `combo-down.svg`, `spin-up.svg` and `spin-down.svg` are used by `GUI_files/styles/atelier.qss`
(through `url(icons:...)`). They are copied unchanged from BildHatcher (`GUI_Files/icons`, drawn for BildHatcher by
its `tools/make_icons.py`), as is the stylesheet itself. The fonts in `GUI_files/fonts` are Plus Jakarta Sans and
Space Mono, under the SIL Open Font License (see the OFL files there).
